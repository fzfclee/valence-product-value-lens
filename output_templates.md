# Output Templates v0.2

## Standard Result

```markdown
# Valence Product Value Lens Result

## Primary Audience
[One precise audience]

## Main Problem
[The user or business problem, not the product feature]

## Expected Change
[The behaviour or outcome expected to change]

## Value Realization Chain
[Audience → problem → change → economic result]

## Primary Economic Outcome
[Grow / Save / Protect]

## Evidence KPI
- [2-4 minimum metrics]

## Directional Monetization
[One formula direction. Mark missing parameters as to confirm.]

## Product Form Question
Why does this need to be [current form]? Could [one or two alternatives] meet the need at lower total cost?

## Data Readiness
[Level 0-4 + one sentence]

## Next Action
[One or two smallest actions]
```

## Insufficient Data Wording

> 当前信息足以形成 Value Realization Chain 草案，但不足以进行可靠价值估算。

Then state:

- what is known;
- the two or three most important data gaps;
- who should confirm them;
- the smallest measurement action.

## Directional Estimate Wording

Use only user-provided parameters.

```markdown
### Directional Estimate
Formula:
User-provided parameters:
Result or range:
Assumptions to confirm:
Confidence limits:
```

Never invent attribution, risk probability, conversion lift, labor cost, or discount factor.

## What Not To Do

Include two or three relevant cautions, for example:

- Do not use all employees as the audience when only selected roles need the product.
- Do not treat logins or page views as economic value.
- Do not call productivity direct cost saving without spend evidence.
- Do not assume a brand-presence need justifies a standalone mini-program.
- Do not attribute all downstream business value to a platform.

## Fixed Closing Boundary

> 这个 Lens 只帮助完成单个产品的价值实现链和数据准备度自查，不替代正式 Valence review、投资决策或产品生命周期治理。
