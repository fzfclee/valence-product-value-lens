# Data Checklists v0.2

Use the smallest data set that can test the Value Realization Chain.

## 1. Common Minimum Data

- Primary Audience and credible denominator;
- core value behaviour;
- baseline before the product or counterfactual without it;
- evidence KPI and data source;
- annual or relevant-period product cost;
- business owner confirmation;
- monetization parameters;
- product-form alternatives.

## 2. Grow

Required where available:

- impacted high-intent audience, leads, or customers;
- baseline conversion or order rate;
- incremental lift;
- unit revenue or contribution margin;
- attribution logic;
- campaign, price, promotion, seasonality, and channel changes.

Do not use total sales as product value.

## 3. Save

### Direct Saving

- baseline actual spend;
- new actual spend;
- vendor, license, material, outsourcing, or budget evidence;
- start date and one-off transition cost.

### Productivity

- active value users or annual transaction volume;
- net labor time saved, not waiting time;
- loaded labor cost;
- adoption or realization factor;
- before/after sample or user confirmation.

Do not call productivity direct saving without spend reduction.

## 4. Protect

- protected revenue, operation, asset, or obligation;
- potential impact range;
- baseline probability or risk tier;
- product control mechanism;
- expected reduction;
- risk, compliance, security, operations, or business owner confirmation.

Prefer ranges when probability is uncertain.

## 5. Branding / Official Presence

- primary high-intent audience;
- official search or channel coverage;
- qualified visits;
- core content completion;
- official next-step or purchase referral;
- attributed conversion where available;
- leakage or revenue-protection hypothesis;
- annual run and compliance cost;
- alternative product forms.

A need for official brand presence does not automatically justify a standalone mini-program.

## 6. Platform / Reuse

- target and active downstream consumers;
- reuse scenarios;
- duplicate builds avoided;
- unit duplicate-build cost;
- onboarding time;
- run cost;
- dependency or continuity evidence.

Separate direct platform value from attributed downstream value.

## 7. Data Readiness Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Product description only |
| 1 | Audience, problem, and expected change can be drafted |
| 2 | Some evidence and monetization parameters support a directional estimate |
| 3 | Evidence, cost, and owner confirmation are ready for formal review |
| 4 | Candidate for full Valence governance review outside the public Lens |
